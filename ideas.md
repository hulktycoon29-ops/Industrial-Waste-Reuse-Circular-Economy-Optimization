# WasteLoop Design Brief

## Direction
Dark industrial operations console for circular-economy decisions, inspired by the supplied reference mockup.

## Design movement
Industrial brutalism softened with modern data-product polish: strong blocks, visible structure, sharp but friendly geometry, and purposeful density.

## Core principles
- Make operational decisions scannable in under 10 seconds.
- Pair every number with units, context, and a visible comparison.
- Treat sustainability metrics as operational outputs, not decorative badges.
- Keep interaction states obvious with mint highlights and clear selected surfaces.

## Color philosophy
The canvas is an almost-black green (`#07130f`) with layered panels (`#0d1d17`, `#10261d`) and hairline borders in muted green. Cream (`#ecf4e7`) carries the main text. Mint/acid green (`#9af5b3`) is the signature action color. Amber and rust are reserved for constraint and risk signals. Blue-green is used for secondary analytics.

## Layout paradigm
A fixed slim sidebar anchors navigation while a responsive content canvas uses a strong page header, summary KPI strip, dense two-column cards, and a right-side detail drawer on wide screens. The intake and comparison views reuse the same shell and visual tokens.

## Signature elements
A compact loop-shaped WasteLoop mark, small uppercase section labels, dotted/grid texture, metric chips, and route/flow line motifs.

## Interaction philosophy
Controls should behave like an instrument panel: immediate updates, no unnecessary confirmation, visible active states, and contextual detail available without losing the current view. Use drawers and compact tabs rather than navigating away for inspectable information.

## Animation
Use restrained 150–220ms transitions for hover, tab, drawer, and progress changes. Avoid decorative motion; a subtle route-line shimmer can support the hero KPI only if it does not distract.

## Typography system
Use a clean sans-serif system stack for legibility, with a heavier display weight for page titles. Numbers use tabular lining figures. Labels are compact uppercase with tracking. No more than three text sizes should compete within one card.

## Brand essence
WasteLoop turns industrial leftovers into measurable next-use value.

## Brand voice
Precise, calm, practical, and evidence-minded. Prefer “estimated emissions change” to vague claims.

## Wordmark/logo
Wordmark: `WasteLoop` with a loop-arrow icon formed from two rounded chevrons and a small negative-space center. The mark should work at 24px and in monochrome.

## Signature brand color
Mint acid green `#9AF5B3`.
